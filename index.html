🌸 Who We Are

A Grand Venue for Every Joyous Occasion

Welcome to Smart Guest House — a spacious, open-air venue nestled at Nidauri Main Gate, Baraon, Karchana, Prayagraj. We offer an elegant setting for weddings, receptions, and all auspicious ceremonies — blending festive warmth with modern comfort.

From a beautifully decorated mandap to marigold-draped aisles and twinkling fairy lights, every corner of our venue is designed to make your celebration truly unforgettable. With ample seating, dedicated catering space, and personalized event support, we take care of every detail so you can savour every moment.
